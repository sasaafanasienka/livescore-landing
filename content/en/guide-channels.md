Live Score

Register a Telegram channel with LiveScoreAppBot for match notifications in the channel.

Open a private chat with the bot and send /channel. Telegram asks you to share a channel with the required post rights. After you share, the bot completes registration when type, rights, and access checks pass — conflicts and failed shares can still fail. Running /channel outside a private chat fails without a share keyboard.

Free: one team or league favourite per registered channel. Chat Premium unlocks more. User-registered notification channels are distinct from official league channels.
