Live Score

What is Live Score?
A Telegram football product: the bot LiveScoreAppBot delivers match notifications, and a Mini App lets you browse matches and manage favourites.

Who is it for?
Personal DMs, groups, and channels. Personal chats get configurable favourites alerts. Groups and channels register through the bot for chat notifications.

What about Chat Premium?
For registered groups and channels only: free for one team or league favourite; Chat Premium unlocks more for 49 Stars, billed for 30 days, with a 24-hour grace period after lapse. Personal chats keep favourites alerts without a Stars upgrade.
