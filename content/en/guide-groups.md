Live Score

Register a Telegram group with LiveScoreAppBot for match notifications in the chat.

Open a private chat with the bot and send /group. Telegram asks you to share a group where the bot and you have the required admin rights. After you share, the bot completes registration when type, rights, and access checks pass — conflicts and failed shares can still fail. Running /group outside a private chat fails without a share keyboard.

Free: one team or league favourite per registered group. Chat Premium unlocks more. This is your group's notification profile — not the official league channels list.
